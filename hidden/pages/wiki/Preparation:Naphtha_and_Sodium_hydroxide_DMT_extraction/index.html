Items needed

  • Tall and thin jar that can hold up to 900ml preferably with a metal lid.
  • Small jar that can hold up to 200ml
  • 100g Mimosa hostilis root bark or Acacia confusa root bark
  • 100g Lye (sodium hydroxide)
  • 600ml Water
  • Turkey baster
  • freezer
  • scalpel
  • safety goggles
  • safety gloves
  • Naptha

Procedure

Step 1 - SAFETY

Safety goggles and safety gloves should be worn while handling lye. Long sleeve tops and trousers should also be worn for extra protection as lye reacts with water and becomes extremely hot. If any of the solution comes into contact with skin or eyes, seek medical advice.

Step 2

The water should be boiled and left to cool and then the lye should be added until the solution becomes clear. (Always add the lye to the water slowly bit by bit and not the other way around, as I already mentioned the lye and water mixed together will become very hot and if the water is added to the lye it could become extremely hot and smash the jar and come into contact with skin.) Once the solution is clear, the mimosa hostilis root bark is then added and submerged in the solution. Then the lid needs to be screwed onto the jar and left at room temperature for 24 hours.

Step 3

After the 24 hours, the naptha should be added into the jar. The lid is then placed back on and the jar needs to be shaken. The jar then needs to left alone for another 24 hours in somewhere heated, such as on top of a radiator. Shake the jar every so often throughout the 24 hours. During this step, the naptha slowly absorbs the dmt from the root bark.

Step 4

A turkey baster is then used to collect the naptha and put it into the 200ml jar. The naptha should be completely clear with a tint of yellow. No brown liquid should be tolerated as this affects the dmt. If your naptha has a tint of browny orange, leave the naptha sit in the separate jar over night. The brown liquid should then separate and should be removed before moving on to the next step.


Step 5

The jar of naptha should be placed in to the freezer for 24 hours. The jar then needs to be taken out of the freezer and left for about half an hour so that the naptha becomes clear. The naptha should be slowly poured back into the jar filled with the root bark and left in the aquarium heater.

Step 6

There should only be crystals with a tint of yellow-white left in the 200ml jar. The jar needs to be left for 24 hours as it needs to dry out. Placing the jar in front of a fan will speed things up. Once the dmt is dried, it can be then scraped out with a clean scalpel or knife.

Dosage, onset and smoking technique

  • Threshold : 2 - 5 mg
  • Light : 10 - 20 mg
  • Moderate : 20 - 40 mg
  • Strong : 40 - 60 mg
  • Onset : 15 - 60 seconds
  • Duration : 5 - 20 minutes
  • Normal After Effects : 15 - 60 minutes
