If you would like to join the proofreading team, please contact Oscarette via her profile page or email oscarettewilde@gmail.com.

Please add the following tag into any article that needs proofreading and editing. It will be added onto this category and promptly dealt with. 

{{proofread}}

The tag will display as:

This article requires proofreading.

As such, it likely contains incorrect grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
