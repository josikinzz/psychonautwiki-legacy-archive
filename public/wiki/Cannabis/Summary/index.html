From PsychonautWiki
Jump to: navigation, search

Main article: Cannabis

Psychoactive class: Hallucinogen

Chemical class: Cannabinoid

Harm potential

    • Mildly habit-forming
    • Extremely low toxicity

Tolerance

    • Full tolerance is reached develops with prolonged and repeated use
    • Decreases to half after 1 - 2 weeks
    • Returns to baseline after 2 - 3 weeks
    • Cross-tolerance with all cannabinoids

Physical effects

    • Changes in gravity
    • Muscle relaxation
    • Spontaneous tactile sensations

    • Appetite enhancement
