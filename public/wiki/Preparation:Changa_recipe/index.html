The following recipe is for 1g of changa at 50% ratio (100mg changa = 50mg DMT)

Required materials

  • 1g of preferred base material (Banisteriopsis Caapi leaf recommended)
  • 500mg DMT
  • 1.5-2g of Harmala alkaloid freebase
  • 20ml of the purest ethanol you can find (preferably 80%+ grain alcohols, like Everclear)
  • 2 small jars or shot glasses

Procedure

  1. Pour out 20ml of alcohol and place it in the shot glass or jar
  2. Measure out 500mg of DMT and place it in ethanol. If it doesn't immediately dissolve, then carefully warm liquid by placing it in a jar and submersing it in warm/hot liquid.
  3. Measure out 1.5-2g of Harmala alkaloids and dissolve in the same way.
  4. Measure out 1g of preferred plant matter, preferably B. Caapi leaf.
  5. Pack the leaf into the bottom of the shot glass or small jar
  6. Pour DMT/Harmala-infused alcohol into the packed shot glass or jar until completely soaked. Then place this on a plate or tray and spread evenly.
  7. Let the leaf completely dry and collect. This is your final product.
