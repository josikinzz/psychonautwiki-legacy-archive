Ranks

    Ranks act as a leveling system which automatically assigns a title to users once they have accrued a specific amount of points under a registered account.

  • Muggle - 0 points
  • Casual User - 500 points
  • Informed User - 1000 points
  • Regular Contributor - 2000 points
  • Hyperspace Explorer - 10000 points
  • DMT Entity - 20000 points
  • Self Transforming Machine Elf - 30000 points
  • Cosmic Pioneer - 40000 points
  • Apprentice Shaman - 60000 points
  • Seasoned Shaman - 100000 points
  • Where is My Mind? - 130000 points
  • I am Everything - 170000 points
  • Officially Transcended - 3,000,000 points
  • Grand Master Shaman of the Universe - ???? points

Awards

Awards are automatically given to users and displayed on their social profile once they have accrued a specific amount of points.

  • 10 edits

  • 100 edits

  • 1000 edits

  • 10,000 edits

Point allocation

    Points are automatically allocated to users when they perform certain tasks across the wiki under a registered account.

    • 20 points awarded for giving a gift
    • 50 points awarded for main namespace edits
    • 50 points awarded for adding a friend
    • 50 points awarded for receiving a gift
    • 60 points awarded for adding a romantic partner
    • 500 points awarded for adding your first avatar
