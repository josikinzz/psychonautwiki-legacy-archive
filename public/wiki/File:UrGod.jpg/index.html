The internet's transcension - A composition piece representing the artists most significant personal influences in regards to the psychedelic experience. It was created by josikins and made to serve as an example of psychedelic artwork

Artistic review by Apoctapus :

"While your color palette is reminiscent of South American Huachuma curanderismos, your figurative studies explore the post-DMT technological aesthetic of the early 2000's. It is a mature digital image, yet retains a playful nature by touching on our divisive alien overlords, mind control and the ever-present appearance that cats have assumed dominance over this reality tunnel.

The stunning absence of third eyes is a bold departure from tradition. At first I felt this was an error, but it posits a delicious question to the viewer. Why, in the visual space of boundary dissolution and ego death, doesn't every thought-form have its own third eye, if any of them have one at all? In the end i agree with Strophalos on this point. The only way to improve this piece is to add more third eyes."

I think, when working with Lawrence Lessig (I suspect is a member of Anonymous as well) as a young teenager, he got involved in some type of situation that was being used against him in order to make him an actual MARTYR. This is, of course, based on nothing but my suspicions. When Aaron Swartz created web.py, I think he opened the door to this new mass media shift. With the legion of Anonymous followers, they have infiltrated the web with biased content in order to change public opinion on public policy. I have seen a surge of blog postings and things like websites offering Alternative Media information, normally against the US government.
