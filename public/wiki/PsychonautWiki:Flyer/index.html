Section 1

About

PsychonautWiki is a community-driven encyclopedia which is striving to formally document the field of psychonautics in a legitimate and comprehensive manner without depending on metaphors, analogies, or personal experience reports.

Our documentation includes an extensive archive of well-organized content regarding the scientific information and harm reduction techniques behind hallucinogenic substance usage and other areas of psychonautics.

This is presented alongside of an integrated subjective effects description index which is capable of breaking down the consistent experiences induced by specific compounds and pharmacological classes into easily readable terminologies, descriptions, leveling systems and example images.

We welcome contributors and encourage those who wish to further our cause to register and collaborate with us.

Mission Statement:

  1. To document all aspects of psychonautics from a skeptical, reason-based and scientific perspective which separates the field from its associated mysticism.
  2. To minimize danger, maximize safety and end the cultural taboos associated with personal psychoactive substance usage by providing unbiased and accurate information.
  3. To serve as a community-driven platform through which ongoing collaboration will be organized and encouraged.

(Revised) About

PsychonautWiki is an online community-driven encyclopedia which is striving to formally document the field of psychonautics in a legitimate manner without depending on metaphors, analogies, or personal experience reports.

Our documentation includes an extensive archive of well-organized content regarding the scientific information and harm reduction techniques behind hallucinogenic substance usage and other areas of psychonautics.

This is presented alongside of an integrated subjective effects index which is capable of breaking down the consistent experiences induced by specific compounds into easily readable terminologies, descriptions, levelling systems and example images.

(Revised) Mission Statement

Our mission is to document all aspects of psychonautics by using a skeptical, reason-based and scientific perspective, successfully separating the field from its associated mysticism. We also work to provide unbiased and accurate information to minimize danger, maximize safety, and end the associated cultural taboos.

(Revised) Contribute

We welcome contributors and encourage those who wish to further our cause to register and collaborate with us. To chat, please visit our IRC channel @ www.psychonautwiki.org:7778
