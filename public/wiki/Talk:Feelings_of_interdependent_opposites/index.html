Tertiary universe

So energy is its own concept, as depicted as the rightmost circle in the mandelbrot set. It is the imprint in spacetime that is left when matter interacts. When two units of matter interact (positive/negative, top left, bottom left of the set respectively), they leave an impression in spacetime (observable energy which ripples throughout the universe in waves). Because this whole process is fractal, the set contains itself in iterations.

This is an image of a quantum dynamic interaction! [speculation]
